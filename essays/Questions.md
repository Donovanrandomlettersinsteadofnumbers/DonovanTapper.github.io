<h3>Smart / Not Smart</h3>
Smart questions in software engineering seem to be important to better filter out the things that can be easily solved from a problem that someone may actually be struggling to solve. A smart question shows that the person asking has already tried finding a solution and gives a detailed explanation to what the problem is. This makes it as easy as possible to involved with the question. While a not smart question looks like something you would write for a google search or to ChatGPT.
